# Emotionally Guarded / Dam Analogy Draft

Imagine being the most emotionally guarded you have ever been and then the universe plays a nasty trick on you. Imagine just realising a week ago that you had healed to a point where you had crossed the line. You had come a little too far. The healing had been done a long long time ago and now that you realize where you were standing, just as that happened she came. She had always been there but there she was, standing in all her glory and all her beauty.

My version:

1. And just as I started to realise and accept the consequences of healing too much, as I previously talked about, being my most emotionally guarded self ever yet, to a point I had not anticipated when I set out on the journey two years ago, the universe decided it was time to have some fun with me. What did it do, you ask? Well, it sent her, again and before I could even understand or analyse as I usually always tend to, there she was, standing in all her glory and all her beauty.

2. For the first time I two years, I did not analyse the situation, I did not think of every possibility there could be, I gave the Doctor Strange in me a break from seeing all possible outcomes (MCU reference) and for the first time in two years, a voice in me just asked me to be present, right there, in that moment, with her. All I know is that I went back home smelling like her and my pinky feeling incomplete without the weight of her ring I took from her. (Hold your horses)

The Dam Analogy

3. And this confuses me, because how can I, at my core be scared, almost resistant for anything related to love or well, women in general—but at the same time feel good when something does actually come my way? That’s an question I don’t have an answer to right now, but all I know is this: for the first time in two years, I found myself in a state of overflow and that is precisely what I did not want. Allow me to explain using an analogy I best use for myself.

4. Imagine you’re in charge of maintaining a dam which supplies water to a village nearby. Your only responsibility in this case would be to disperse the right amount of water so you don’t end up flooding the villagers, regardless of the volume of water the dam really holds. In short, avoid overflow at all costs. But in my case, I am the dam and the villagers is the girl on the other side. And all that massive volume of water? It’s all the love I have of inside me, and trust me I have a lot of it. It’s always been around me, inside me and it’s what fuels me, and I have, in the past, always made the naive mistake of letting it all flow— which in actual context is a good thing because who doesn’t like being loved in ways most people cant comprehend, but thats just the thing, when it comes out in a state of constant overflow, it drowns the villagers regardless of their ability to swim or really require that much water. It was never controlled, and I thought i  could control it, and I have, I’m doing better now than I would have two years ago so clearly the healing did its thing, but not being able to express it or let it all flow at once is what also drives me crazy because thats the only way I know how to love or express when I like someone.

5. Coming off as too strong as they call it these days, and this is precisely one of the reasons why I did not want to get into all of this again, because I know, at my core, that no matter how much I train myself, no matter how much I tell myself to be restricted, to be controlled, to let the water flow one at a time, there are times when I will still just open up. It’s inevitable but I’m working on it: working on not making the other person drown in love they are not yet ready to receive.

6. I realised that no matter how logical, calculative, practical you make yourself, when it comes down to loving again, when it comes down to feeling again, emotions will always take precedence over logic. Emotions will always fuck your logic up, no matter how hard you build to perfect it, or at least, that’s how it works with me. I can be careful, for sure, yes, but at the end of the day, I think I have realized that no matter how much I try to control myself, one day, when I fall in love, I will go crazy. It’s almost inevitable because Itish not going crazy when in love is it like the sun refusing to rise or the moon refusing to shine its light.  Maybe even me writing about it is me overflowing, who knows, but at the same time, this is who I am at my core, and this is who I always will be. So here goes, (you can let go of the horses now)
