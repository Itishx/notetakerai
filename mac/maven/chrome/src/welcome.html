Maven for Chrome

Talk instead of typing, in any text box on the web.

1. Let Maven hear you

Chrome asks once. Maven only listens while you hold a take, and audio is never kept.

2. The two shortcuts

⌃⌥WriteControl + Option. Press, talk, press again. It writes at your cursor, cleaned up.
⌃⌘DoControl + Command. Ask for one small job: “open YouTube”, “go to my Gmail tab”, “fill this form”.

Enter says yes to a card, Esc cancels. Works on web pages, not on Chrome's own pages (new tab, settings).

3. Your details, for forms

Saved only in this browser. Used when you say “fill this form”.
