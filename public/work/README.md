# Braino at work — clip slots

Drop an .mp4 in here with one of these names and it appears in the
"Watch him work" tab on the homepage. Nothing else to change.

  voice.mp4      You talk. He moves.
  delegate.mp4   He runs the errand.
  page.mp4       He reads what you are reading.
  meetings.mp4   He sits in and takes the notes.
  distra.mp4     He writes the week's posts.
  roam.mp4       He walks onto the page.

Until a file exists the tile renders as a labelled slot, so the section is
never broken by a missing clip.

Clips are muted, looped, and play on hover, so they are short by design:
five to ten seconds, 16:9, and readable without sound.
