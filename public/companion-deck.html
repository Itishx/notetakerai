What people use today

Three options. Every one of them waits for you.

Option one

Track it yourself

Streaks, Habitica, Notion, Todoist.

  • A grid you tick and a list you keep
  • You do the logging, every single day
  • Break the streak and 44% never come back
Option two

Block and measure

Freedom, Forest, Rize, Screen Time.

  • Blunt blocking, or a report after the fact
  • No idea what you were trying to do
  • It tells you the week went badly. Nothing else
Option three

Pay a person

A coach, a therapist, an accountability partner.

  • It works, and everybody knows it works
  • $100 an hour, once a week at best
  • Not there at 3pm on a Tuesday, which is when you need it
All three need you to show up, remember, and type. The person who has already run out of discipline is the person being asked to supply it.
