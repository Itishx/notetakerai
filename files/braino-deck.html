What problem are you solving?

Founders · Braino Foundry

You shipped it. Nobody saw it.

  • Building stopped being the bottleneck in 2025

Distribution should be as easy as vibe coding. That sentence is the whole company. Every tool on the market helps you make the thing. Almost nothing helps you get it in front of a human, and the founder is the one person with no time to learn how.

Product 5 / 9

The actual product

Distribution is not one job.
It is six.

01RunningContentPosts everywhere, in your voice, from what you shipped that week.
02NextOpticsGets you named when someone asks an AI for a tool like yours.
03PlannedOutboundFinds the right people and writes to them like a person would.
04PlannedInboundAnswers at midnight, qualifies, and books the meeting.
05PlannedResearchListens for people describing your problem right now.
06PlannedGlueMakes the other five talk. This is the part nobody sells.

Every engine is the same four steps, connect then decide then do then check, so the spine gets built once and each new channel is a thin adapter. Content publishes today on X and Reddit, and Reddit goes through the founder's own browser with no API key at all. Five tools that do not know about each other is the real problem. Glue is the moat.
