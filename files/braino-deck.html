Primary research 3 / 12

Status quo · how founders solve it today

Three ways to do it now. All three fail the same founder.

Grind it by hand

Post, scroll, comment, DM, repeat. The documented case: six months, two hours a day on Reddit, for two or three usable openings a week.

Gap: it costs the exact week they needed to build

Build your own

Sai built a bot called Lara to watch X for him rather than pay for a tool. One channel, one purpose, no maintenance, nobody to call.

Gap: duct tape, and it only ever covers one channel

Hire a human

A GTM consultant was $60 to $70 an hour two years ago. The same person now quotes $700 to $1,000, with a two week lag and no product context.

Gap: priced for companies with revenue. That is not this founder
The gap that changed the plan Zero of eight pay for a single distribution tool. There is nothing to switch them from. Every product on the market is sold to a marketer with a budget. Nobody sells to the pre-revenue founder doing it alone, so Braino is not a replacement, it is the first system they will ever own. Slower to win. Far harder to lose.
