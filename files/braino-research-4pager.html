Secondary research, and the competition.

What the internet confirms — every claim sourced — and why nobody occupies Braino’s seat.

Building is solved. Distribution is the bottleneck.

  • 1 in 3 new companies in 2026 is founded by one person — AI cut dev cost ~90% and time-to-MVP ~60%. (Solo Founder Playbook 2026)
  • A Product Hunt Top-5 finish now brings ~1,500 visits, not 15,000. The old launch playbook is dead.
  • A VC analysis of founder forums found ONE recurring theme: distribution — not AI, not features. (FundnAI)
  • The “Distribution Chasm”: months of building, then launch to silence — five waitlist signups. (Before Takeoff)

The manual grind, quantified.

  • Documented case: 2+ hours daily on Reddit for six months → only 2-3 usable opportunities a week. (r/buildinpublic via VibeCom)
  • That’s ~10 hours of grinding per handful of real conversations — across Reddit, LinkedIn and X alike.
  • Value-first posts earn ~10x the engagement of promotional ones. It works — but eats time founders don’t have.

The AEO shift.

  • 37% of product discovery now STARTS in AI interfaces like ChatGPT and Perplexity. (Profound)
  • ChatGPT referrals convert at 14-16% vs ~1.8% for Google organic — roughly 9x. (Omnibound)
  • Only 18% of brands have any AI-visibility strategy — 82% are invisible by default. (Erlin, 200+ marketing leaders)
  • Citations churn ~23%/month; only ~30% of brands stay visible answer-to-answer — weekly maintenance is mandatory, which makes it recurring revenue. (AirOps)
  • Reddit is ~10% of AI citations; brand websites only 5-10% — multi-platform content does double duty: community reach AND AI visibility. (Semrush, 100M citations)
