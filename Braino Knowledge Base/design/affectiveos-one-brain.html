One brain. Two speeds.

AffectiveOS is one mind. It has Intent detection (Laya) that understands what you want in a blink, on your Mac, and the Human Intelligence Layer (HIL) that plans the hard things. Both see the same world and share one memory, and HIL teaches Laya. Everything else is his hands.

How a sentence travels

After, one brain

  • Intent detection (Laya) makes the one decision, with full context
  • Human Intelligence Layer (HIL) plans the big jobs and settles the unsure ones
  • The 12 checkers become hands the brain calls, not bosses
  • One chat, one memory: slow chat folds into Talk
  • The loop runs only inside a planned job, with a 20 second stop
  • No second opinions: HIL is the bigger brain, not a shadow

Three rules that keep it one brain

  1. Intent detection never overrules HIL. Unsure means hand over, never guess and never "say that again" to a clear sentence.
  2. HIL teaches Laya. Every sentence HIL settles becomes a lesson for Laya, so over time fewer sentences need the slow path.
  3. One picture of the world. Both parts read the same context and the same memory, built once, never fetched twice.
